Privacy

Last updated 10 November 2026.

Spiquizzy runs this site. You can write to us at contact@spiquizzy.com.

What we collect

Cookieless analytics, stored in our own Supabase database: the page path, the referrer hostname, UTM tags, a device type (mobile, tablet or desktop), and whether the event was a view, a play, a solve or a share. We do not store IP addresses, account IDs or cookies for this, and we skip it when your browser sends Do Not Track.

Anonymous daily scores: the game, the difficulty, the date, your time and your move count. There is no name attached.

This device keeps progress and settings in localStorage. The cookie page lists every key.

In future, advertising cookies from Google AdSense would be set only after you consent.

Why

To run the games, to rank times, and to see which pages are used so we can improve the site.

Who else is involved

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Supabase stores the analytics rows and the anonymous scores.
  • Later, Google AdSense and its partners may serve ads. Their list is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

How long we keep it

Analytics and scores are kept for up to 24 months. localStorage stays on your device until you clear it.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of personal data we hold, ask us to erase it, object to how we use it, and ask us to correct it or to restrict it. Email contact@spiquizzy.com. We do not use this data to make automated decisions about you.

Complaints

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Children

We collect no personal data from players, and we do no tracking without consent, in line with the ICO Children's Code.
