Games like Wordle

Ravioli is the word game on Spiquizzy. It is not Wordle. A search for games like Wordle usually means a short daily word puzzle. Ravioli is that kind of appointment, and a different kind of puzzle. This page says what it is and what it is not. It does not include Wordle answers, letter counts, or anything published by The New York Times. Spiquizzy is not affiliated with the Times.

It does not play like Wordle

In a hidden-word game you guess a five-letter word and the tiles tell you which letters are right. Ravioli does none of that. There is no hidden word, no keyboard, and no green, yellow, or gray letter tiles. You see a set of halves, each one a piece of a longer word, and you tap two that join into one real word. Sun and flower make sunflower. That pair is a teaching example from the how to play page, not a tile from today's board. Today's halves are different, and they are not printed here.

A correct pair locks. A wrong pair shakes, and the note tells you those halves are not one of today's words. You do not lose the game for a miss. You try a different half. When every pair is locked, you win. Easy, medium, and hard change how many words are on the table. Everyone on the same local date gets the same set. There is no account. Your streak lives in this browser.

Why people still compare them

People who like a morning word game often like Ravioli for the same outer reasons: it is the same puzzle for everyone, it is over in a few minutes, and you can tell a friend you finished without spoiling the board. The share card shows your time, a row of squares, and how you compared with other Spiquizzy players. It does not list the words. If you want to talk about a pair, do that after your friends have played.

It is a poor fit if you specifically want letter-by-letter deduction. Ravioli is closer to a matching puzzle that happens to use English words. You are looking at both pieces the whole time. The skill is seeing the compound, the name, or the phrase the two halves make, and ignoring the pairs that almost work. A wrong pair does not mark individual letters, so it will not teach spelling the way a guess-the-word game does. It shows you which two pieces do not belong together, in one sentence.

If you wanted a different puzzle

The rest of Spiquizzy is not a word game. Sauce Pipes, Pasta Pips, Slip, Twirl, and Even Bowls are logic puzzles on the same daily clock. Pasta Pips is the domino board, if you arrived from a Pips search and wanted a grid instead of words. The games like Pips page is about that grid, and it is just as clear that this site is not the Times. The free daily puzzles page is the menu for all six.

Play today's Ravioli when you want the word game. Read the how-to if you want the three steps before the first tap. And if you wanted Wordle itself, that game is published by the Times. This site does not reprint it, and it does not keep a list of answers.
