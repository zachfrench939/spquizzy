How to play

Cover the board with dominoes so every coloured sauce tells the truth.

  • A number means the pips in that sauce add up to it.
  • == means every pip in that sauce matches.
  • >2 means every pip is greater than 2.
  • <5 means every pip is less than 5.

Tap a domino to pick it up. Tap it again to swap which half is on the left. Then tap two neighbouring squares. The left half lands on the first square. Tap a placed domino to lift it back off. Each placement counts.
