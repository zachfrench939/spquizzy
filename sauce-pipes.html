How to play

Tap a pipe to turn it one quarter clockwise. Sauce runs out of the pot along every open connection.

Get it onto the plate. Each turn counts, and par is a tidy number for today's board. The clock starts on your first turn.

Easy is a 5×5 plate, medium 6×6, hard 7×7. Same puzzle for everyone on this date.
