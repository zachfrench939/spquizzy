How to play

Each tile is half a word. Tap two halves that join into one common compound, like SUN and FLOWER.

A real pair locks. Anything else shakes off. Each attempt is one try, and par is the number of words on the plate.

Easy is four words, medium five, hard six. Today's pairs are the only ones that clear the board.
