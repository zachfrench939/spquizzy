How to play Ravioli

Ravioli is a daily word puzzle. You see halves of words and tap two that join into one real word. It is not a hidden five-letter guess, and it does not color letters.

Steps

  1. Tap one half. Then tap the half that finishes the word.
  2. A real word stays. Sun and flower make sunflower. That pair is a teaching example, not today's board, and a real pair locks.
  3. A wrong pair shakes. It does not lock. The note says those halves are not one of today's words.

Tips

  • You are looking at both pieces the whole time. There is no hidden word to deduce letter by letter.
  • A miss does not end the game. Pick a different half.
  • The sample pair on this page, sun and flower, is only here to show the join. Today's halves are dealt from the date and are not listed here.
  • Your share card shows a time and squares. It does not list the words.
