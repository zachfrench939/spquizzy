How to play Slip

Slip is a daily sliding puzzle. Blocks of pasta fill a small tray. The meatball has to reach the green exit gate, one square at a time.

Steps

  1. Tap a block. The meatball starts selected when it can move. Tap another block if you want to move that one.
  2. Tap an arrow. Each tap slides the selected block one square, when that square is empty.
  3. Reach the exit gate. Slide the meatball onto the square by the green exit gate.

Tips

  • If every arrow says the way is blocked, the note is right. Tap a different block and clear a path.
  • A move is one square, not a slide across the whole row.
  • The green gate sits just outside the tray. That is the only exit.
  • You can undo a slide. The board does not reset your streak unless you are playing today's puzzle and you win it.
