How to play Pasta Pips

Pasta Pips is a daily domino puzzle. The board is split into colored areas. Each area has a label. Your tray holds the dominoes that cover the board.

Steps

  1. Pick up a domino. Tap it, then tap two squares that share a side.
  2. Read the label. Add to 8 means the dots in that color add up to 8. All same means every dot in that color matches.
  3. Fill the board. A green edge means that color is right. A red edge means it is full but wrong.

Tips

  • The first square you tap gets the first half of the domino. Tap the domino again before placing it if you need the numbers swapped.
  • Two squares have to share a side. A diagonal does not count.
  • A red edge means the area is full and the label failed. Lift that domino and try a different pair of squares.
  • This page does not show today's board. The labels change with the date.
