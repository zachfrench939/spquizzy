How to play Pasta Pips

Pasta Pips is a daily domino puzzle. The board is split into coloured areas. Each area has a label. Your tray holds the dominoes that cover the board.

Steps

  1. Place a domino. Drag a domino from the tray onto the board. It snaps to the nearest two squares that share a side. Tap a placed domino to turn it, or drag it off the board to put it back. With a keyboard, press a domino, then press two squares.
  2. Read the label. “Add to 3” means the dots in that colour add up to 3. “All same” means every half in that colour shows the same number. “Over” and “Under” mean the total is above or below the number.
  3. Green is right, red is wrong. A green edge means that area is complete and correct. A red edge means it is full but the label failed — drag that domino off and try again.

Tips

  • The left half of the domino lands on the left square, or the top half lands on the top square. Tap the domino in the tray before you drag if you need the numbers swapped.
  • Two squares have to share a side. A diagonal does not count.
  • A red edge means the area is full and the label failed. Drag that domino off the board and try a different pair of squares.
  • This page does not show today's board. The labels change with the date.
