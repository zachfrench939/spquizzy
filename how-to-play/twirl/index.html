How to play Twirl

Twirl is a daily path puzzle. Matching pictures sit on a grid. You draw a line from one picture to its twin, and the lines together have to cover every square.

Steps

  1. Match the pictures. Press a picture and drag to the same picture.
  2. Stay on empty squares. The line cannot cross another line or another picture.
  3. Cover every square. You win when every square is part of a line.

Tips

  • A tap is not a line. Press and drag, and stay on neighbouring squares. If you let go early, the note tells you to drag to the matching picture.
  • Pictures that are not the pair you started from are walls. You cannot pass through them.
  • If two paths would have to cross, one of them needs a different route.
  • The pictures are garlic, cheese, basil, olives, and the other foods in the set. Match the shape, not only the color.
