How to play Sauce Pipes

Sauce Pipes is a daily path puzzle. The pot and the plate are fixed. Every other tile is a pipe you can turn. You win when red sauce runs from the pot to the plate.

Steps

  1. Turn a pipe. Tap a pipe. Each tap turns it a quarter turn.
  2. Follow the red. Sauce only travels where openings meet. If a pipe does not face its neighbour, the path stops.
  3. Reach the plate. You win when the sauce touches the plate.

Tips

  • Start with the pipe that touches the pot. If that opening does not face the pot, the rest of the board stays empty.
  • Sauce only travels through openings that meet. A corner, a straight, a T, and a cross are the four shapes, and turning one changes which sides are open.
  • The plate does not need a long path. The moment the sauce touches it, the puzzle is done.
  • Easy, medium, and hard are three sizes of the same day. Everyone on your local date shares those boards.
